# MICHI beta playtest

MICHI teaches you to solve Sudoku one logical step at a time. Start as a guest, try a Daily, and use a puzzle of your own if you have one. Stop when you have enough to tell us what helped or confused you.

- [ ] Play today’s Sudoku until you want help. Where, if anywhere, did Coach give you just enough information to continue?
- [ ] Try **Why?** on a move. What did you think it explained?
- [ ] Choose a technique in Learn and try its practice exercise. Was it clear how to get from the explanation to applying it yourself?
- [ ] Scan a printed Sudoku under your usual lighting. Which digits, if any, needed correction in Review?
- [ ] Scan a partially played puzzle where you are stuck and choose **I’m stuck — help me**. Could you use that one step back on paper?
- [ ] Move between playing, learning and Progress. Where did you lose your place or look for something twice?
- [ ] Would you come back for tomorrow’s Daily? What would bring you back?

For a problem, tell us what you tapped, what you expected, what happened, your phone model, language and build number. Screenshots are optional. Crop out personal information. Use the feedback channel supplied with the invitation.
